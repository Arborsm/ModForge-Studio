Soft Frame

This final mark keeps the Mod Cell idea and reduces the heavy dark border. The outer shape is a narrow structural frame with a light inset panel, so the cell reads cleaner and the mark feels less blocky.

The old offset blob shadow has been replaced with a centered soft cast shadow, removing the dirty-looking lower-left patch while keeping depth under the mark.
